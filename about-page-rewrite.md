## Short Intro

I’m PJ Haft, a Lead UX Designer focused on turning complex technical capabilities into product experiences people can understand and use. I work across discovery, research synthesis, information architecture, prototyping, design systems, and shipped enterprise software.

I’m most interested in the layer where data, automation, and real people meet: the moment a product needs to explain itself, guide a decision, reduce friction, or help someone move with confidence.

## Values

My approach is guided by three things:

- Clarity: make complex systems easier to understand.
- Trust: design data-rich experiences that feel transparent and controllable.
- Craft: bring enough visual and interaction polish that powerful software still feels considered.
