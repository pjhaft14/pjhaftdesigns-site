# Rikidozan Shirt Production Notes

## Approved Concept

- Primary garment: natural / unbleached cotton
- Alternate garment: washed black or faded charcoal
- Front: distressed red Puroresu stamp with the authentic square logo knocked out
- Front placement: wearer's left chest
- Front size: 2.5-2.75 inches tall (approximately 1.3-1.45 inches wide)
- Back: Rikidozan Showa poster
- Back size: approximately 12 x 15 inches

## Print Assets

- Front stamp: `puroresu-red-knockout-stamp.png` (391 x 744 px)
- Back print: `rikidozan-back-print-mockup.png`
- Editable mockup: `rikidozan-shirt-front-back-mockup.psd`

## DTG Notes

- The front stamp resolves to about 298 PPI at 2.5 inches tall and 271 PPI at 2.75 inches tall.
- The current stamp is suitable for the natural-shirt colorway.
- Keep the knocked-out characters and major distress openings clearly separated at final size.
- For washed black, prepare and sample a dark-garment version because the white underbase can affect semi-transparent distressed edges.
- Export production artwork in sRGB and order a physical sample before inventory production.
