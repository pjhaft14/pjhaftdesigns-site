A little about me.

Outside of work, I love traveling with my wife, spending time with our dog Mylo, tending to my bonsai trees, and following Liverpool. There's usually a trip to plan, a tree to check on, or a match to watch.

Japanese culture and professional wrestling have been interests of mine for years. They eventually led me to build the Puroresu Project, where I get to combine design with wrestling history. It's a personal project that gives me plenty of reasons to keep learning and making things.
